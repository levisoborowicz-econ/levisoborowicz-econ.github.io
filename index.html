Welcome

Welcome to my academic website. I earned my PhD in Economics from Iowa State University in August 2024, following my dual undergraduate degree in Economics and Mathematics from the University of Wisconsin-Eau Claire (2019). My research interests incorporate Industrial Organization primarily, and elements of Transportation Economics, International Trade, Labor Economics, and Environmental Economics.

Currently, I serve as a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Environmental Economics group at the Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences, working on a project titled Scrap and Grab: Should Sweden introduce a vehicle scrappage subsidy?

Research Interests

  • Industrial Organization - Market structures, Demand Estimation, and Policy Counterfactuals
  • Transportation Economics - Trucking markets, Maritime Transport, and Green Vehicles
  • Labor Economics - Occupational Choice, Wage Determination
  • Environmental Economics - Sustainable transportation, Policy Evaluation, Carbon Emissions
